AI security

Your farm, protected around the clock

Our AI camera system uses YOLOv8, the same technology used in commercial security, to detect people and animals the moment they enter your farm.

  • Detects humans, dogs, cats, and birds in real time.
  • Highlights close-range intrusions with visible alerts.
  • Saves snapshots with timestamped event records.
  • Runs offline with no internet and no monthly fees.
